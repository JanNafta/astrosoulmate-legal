AstroSoulmate · Love Align

Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 August 2026 · Applies to AstroSoulmate version 2.0 and later

The short version. There is no account, no sign in and no server of ours. Your birth details and your readings never leave your iPhone. The only thing the app shares is anonymous usage statistics (which features are used), collected through Google Firebase Analytics with tracking disabled, as described in section 6. You can erase your data and reset the analytics identifier from Settings with one tap.

AstroSoulmate: Love Align is developed and published by Jan Daniel Naftanaila, an individual developer registered with Apple under team H28XDPBVAK. Contact: support@digitalnafta.com.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not create an account for you and we do not ask for an email address.
  • We do not run a server of our own that receives your data. Your birth details and your readings are never uploaded anywhere.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell or share anything with data brokers.
  • We do not embed advertising SDKs. The only third party SDK in the app is Google Firebase Analytics, used solely for the anonymous usage statistics described in section 6.
  • We do not collect biometric data. The palm reading is derived from your birth chart, not from an image: no photo is taken and no biometric template is created, stored or compared.
  • Your conversations with Astro Chat are not used to train any AI model.

4. How your readings are produced

Your natal chart is computed on your device with an astronomical engine built into the app, using established algorithms for the positions of the Sun, the Moon and the planets. The readings are written by us in advance and selected deterministically from your chart and the date, so the same chart on the same day always produces the same text. Nothing is sent anywhere to generate them.

The soulmate portrait is an artistic illustration selected from artwork bundled inside the app. It is labelled in the interface as an AI-generated artistic portrait and it is not a photograph, a prediction or a depiction of any real person.

5. Camera and photos

The app never opens the camera and never reads your photo library. The palm reading is produced from your birth chart, as described above, without any image. The only photo permission the app can request is add-only access, used exclusively when you tap Save to Photos to store your soulmate card in your library.

6. Anonymous usage analytics

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics (in its variant without advertising identifier support) to understand which features are used, so we can improve the app. This is the only data that leaves your device, and it works like this:

  • What is sent. Event names such as "horoscope viewed" or "reading saved", the screen involved, coarse device information (model, iOS version, language, country) and a random per-install identifier generated by Firebase. That identifier is not linked to your identity: there is no account, and we never receive your name, your birth details or the content of any reading.
  • What is not sent. No advertising identifier, no location, no contacts, no photos, no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • Who processes it. Google LLC acts as our processor. Google may process this data on servers outside your country, including the United States, under the safeguards Google offers for Firebase services, including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.
  • Retention. Event data tied to the per-install identifier is retained by Firebase for a limited period (at most 14 months) and aggregated statistics are kept beyond that without any identifier.
  • Legal basis. Where the GDPR applies, this processing rests on our legitimate interest in understanding aggregate feature usage (Article 6(1)(f)). It involves no profiling of you as a person and has no legal effect on you.
  • Your opt-out. Settings, Your data, Delete my data resets the analytics identifier on the spot, disconnecting any previously sent events from your install. Deleting the app has the same effect.

7. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app whether an entitlement is active, and nothing more. Apple's handling of that transaction is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

8. Notifications

Notifications are scheduled locally by your iPhone. There is no push server and no device token is sent anywhere. You can turn each type off individually inside the app or in iOS Settings.

9. Your control

  • See it. Settings, Your data, Export my data produces a readable JSON file with your profile and the local analytics buffer.
  • Erase it. Settings, Your data, Delete my data removes the file immediately, resets the anonymous analytics identifier and returns the app to its first run state. Deleting the app from your iPhone has the same effect.
  • There is no account to close and no request form to fill in.

10. Children

AstroSoulmate is rated for ages 12 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. The only data the app collects is the anonymous usage statistics described in section 6, which contain no personal details and cannot identify anyone.

11. Legal bases and your rights

Where the GDPR applies: your birth details and readings are processed entirely on your own device under your control, and the anonymous usage analytics described in section 6 rest on our legitimate interest, with Google LLC as processor. You retain the rights of access, rectification, erasure and objection, and you can exercise all of them directly in the app without contacting us: export shows the data, delete removes it and resets the analytics identifier. You can also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. Where the CCPA applies, we do not sell or share personal information, and we have not done so in the preceding twelve months.
