Support

Last updated 10 August 2026 · Applies to AstroSoulmate version 2.0 and later

Get help

Write to support@digitalnafta.com and describe what happened. Include your iPhone model and your iOS version if the app misbehaved.

Common questions

I do not know my exact birth time. Can I still use the app?

Yes. Turn on "I don't know my birth time" during setup. The app then computes everything that does not depend on the exact hour: your Sun, your Moon, the planets, aspects, compatibility and all readings. What you will not see is your Rising sign and the house positions, because both of those change every few minutes and cannot be guessed honestly.

How do I change my birth details?

Settings, Your data, Birth details.

How do I delete everything?

Settings, Your data, Delete my data. It takes effect immediately and there is nothing left to delete anywhere else, because nothing ever left your iPhone.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then AstroSoulmate. You can also reach it from Settings, Manage subscription inside the app. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I bought premium but the app shows me the paywall.

Tap Restore Purchases in Settings. If it still does not unlock, make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy, then write to us.

Is my palm photo stored anywhere?

No. It is analysed in memory while you look at the result and is then released. It is never written to disk and never uploaded.

Is the astrology real?

The astronomy is real: planetary positions are computed on your device with established algorithms and are accurate to a few arcminutes, which is far more than enough for signs, houses and aspects. The interpretation on top of that astronomy is entertainment, and the app says so on every reading.
